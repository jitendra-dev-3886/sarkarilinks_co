# Homepage reference review and monetization plan

Reviewed 6 September 2026. This is an original implementation; no competitor content, logos, assets or templates were copied. No traffic or earnings data was supplied for either site.

## What the references suggest

[Sarkari Link](https://sarkarilink.com/) puts search, category navigation and separate notice lists on the homepage. [SarkariResult.com.cm](https://sarkariresult.com.cm/) gives prominent access to results, admit cards and job lists, plus tools and a channel link. These patterns suggest visitors often arrive with a specific exam task. Their branding or claims of official status have not been independently verified.

Our homepage now adds a three-column exam update desk for results, admit cards and answer keys, ahead of the existing job cards. It uses published database records, publication dates, English/Hindi selection, refresh/retry controls and category links. Mobile collapses into one column. Five administrator-selected designs remain available. Existing saved jobs, preference matching, closing-soon ordering and recent tools provide repeat-use shortcuts.

The design hypothesis is that relevant, fresh notices plus useful application tools will encourage return visits. It needs measurement: no design will appeal to everyone. [NN/g recommends evaluating repeat visits rather than trying to manufacture extra page clicks](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/return-visits-not-bounce/).

## Practical route to revenue

| Option | What must exist first | First experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clearly labeled direct sponsorship | A relevant sponsor, audience evidence, agreed placement and disclosure | Offer one fixed-term sponsored placement below useful notice content; manually agree terms before building billing |
| Display advertising | A live, substantive site, original useful content and network approval | After approval, test one reserved-size ad below the notice board; compare revenue and repeat visits |
| Optional paid assistance | A real service you can deliver, transparent pricing and support | Test demand for human resume review or application-document assistance; keep government links and existing free utilities free |
| Relevant affiliate recommendations | Accepted affiliate relationship and honest disclosure | One genuinely useful preparation resource in a relevant guide; track conversions without altering editorial rankings |

These are proposed experiments, not installed payment or ad integrations. No ad account, sponsor agreement, checkout, affiliate ID or tracking service has been configured. Do not present blank ad placeholders or invented sponsors to visitors.

[Google's ad placement guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1282097?hl=en) stresses clear separation from content and navigation and avoiding accidental clicks. An ad must not resemble an official Apply, Result or Download button. Keep publisher content central and reserve ad dimensions to limit page movement. Public notice publishing and editorial independence must remain separate from sponsorship.

## First four weeks: sequence, not an earnings promise

1. **Content and trust:** publish accurate source-linked notices in a manageable exam/state niche. Add useful eligibility explanations, document checklists and corrections. Complete About, contact, privacy and editorial information with real business details before monetization applications.
2. **Discoverability:** verify the production domain, canonical URLs and sitemap in Search Console. Create useful original guides around actual reader questions. Avoid generating large numbers of thin pages from OCR or keyword variations.
3. **Repeat visits:** check which notices and tools people return for. Add opt-in alerts only after a delivery service, unsubscribe handling and consent flow work; no inert Subscribe button. Alerts are not implemented in this release.
4. **One revenue experiment:** use actual audience figures to approach a relevant sponsor, or apply to an ad network when the site is ready. Review outcomes before increasing commercial placements.

Track 7/28-day returning-user rates, repeat visits to saved jobs, successful tool exports, official-source clicks, search queries with no results, mobile performance, net revenue and support complaints. Analytics is not installed by this change. Choose a measurement service and appropriate privacy setup before collecting visitor data; never include uploaded files, OCR text, resumes or private form values in analytics.

For planning, monthly display-ad revenue = monthly pageviews / 1,000 × measured page RPM. This is a calculation, not a forecast. Net earnings subtract hosting, processing, editorial and support costs. Without traffic and an actual RPM there is no responsible income estimate or reliable fast-earnings timeline.

## Validation

Run the frontend build and production browser tests. Check that each board column only contains its category, Hindi selection handles missing translations honestly, refresh retrieves current data, and all five designs reflow on mobile. Existing publication rules still exclude drafts and expired notices. A visual reference review is not proof of competitor superiority or revenue uplift.
